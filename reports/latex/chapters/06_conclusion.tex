\section{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

Single-trial classification of targets versus standards is highly accurate (RQ1): median balanced accuracy 0.949 within participants (\code{xdawn_rg}) and 0.924 for unseen participants (EEGNet). Deep learning did not outperform the classical pipeline within participants, but was significantly better across participants (RQ2). The features learned by EEGNet are interpretable (RQ3): they reproduce the centro-parietal P3b, with a smaller contribution from motor preparation before the button press that an early time window alone does not remove.
