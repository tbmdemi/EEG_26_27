\section{Preliminary conclusions and remaining work}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\textbf{Preliminary answers.} Single-trial classification of targets versus standards is highly accurate (RQ1): median balanced accuracy 0.949 within participants (\code{xdawn_rg}) and 0.924 for unseen participants (EEGNet). Deep learning did not outperform the classical pipeline within participants, but was significantly better across participants (RQ2). The features learned by EEGNet are interpretable (RQ3): they reproduce the centro-parietal P3b, with a smaller contribution from motor preparation before the button press that an early time window alone does not remove.

\textbf{Completed.} Preprocessing of all 42 participants and data release v2; behavioural check against the original study; ERPs, scalp maps and P300 peaks; all four models on the main task in both settings and windows; both classical models and EEGNet on the two control tasks; RQ2 statistics; EEGNet and LDA explanations; this report draft.
