\section{Data}
\label{ch:data}

We used the visual oddball task of the Nencki-Symfonia dataset \citep{dzianok2022}, OpenNeuro ds004621 \citep{dzianok2022data}: 42 healthy right-handed adults (20 to 34 years), 127 EEG channels (actiCHamp, 1000~Hz, online reference FCz) with digitised electrode positions. Each participant saw 660 stimuli: standards (75\%), targets (12\%) and distractors (12\%), shown for 200~ms every 1.4 to 1.8~s, and pressed a key with the right hand for targets only.

A target was scored correct if a response followed 100 to 1400~ms after onset (its reaction time, RT, is the first response); a standard or distractor was correct if no response followed. Only correct trials were analysed. With this rule our behavioural results for the 39 participants left after the a priori exclusions reproduce Table~5 of the original study to within 0.03 percentage points (hit rate 94.4\%, mean RT 542~ms). Three participants were excluded a priori because they misunderstood the task (sub-02, sub-14, sub-17), and four because fewer than 50\% of their correct targets survived artefact rejection (sub-08, sub-23, sub-35, sub-41). The final cohort has 35 participants and 20,600 epochs (15,744 standards, 2,477 targets, 2,379 distractors).
